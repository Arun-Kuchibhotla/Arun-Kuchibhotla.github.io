Arun Kumar Kuchibhotla

Evergreen Junior Career Professor · 2024–2027

I am an associate professor in the Department of Statistics & Data Science at Carnegie Mellon University, where I study how to quantify uncertainty when standard statistical assumptions are difficult to justify.

For example, researchers often use the same data to choose a regression model and estimate its coefficients. I develop confidence intervals that account for this choice. I also study how to obtain valid coverage when observations are dependent or estimators have nonstandard limiting distributions.

Background

I joined Carnegie Mellon in 2020 after completing my PhD in Statistics at the Wharton School under the guidance of Lawrence D. Brown and Andreas Buja. I earned my bachelor’s and master’s degrees in Statistics at the Indian Statistical Institute.

In 2026, I received an Alfred P. Sloan Research Fellowship and the Gottfried E. Noether Early Career Scholar Award.
